# Innovation candidates (research only)

Branch `research/manufacturing-innovation-v2`, branched from the frozen tag `manufacturing-demo-v1.0` (`c25466c56e5724158d534ff7b59f01ac48643101`). This document changes no code, no architecture and nothing in the frozen baseline.

**Status of this list:** six provisional research directions, recorded in the order given. They are **not selected, not ranked and not frozen**. For every direction, **novelty, feasibility and differentiation are unverified**: no prior-art search, technical spike or comparison has been done. The owner will decide only after all six have been discussed.

## 1. Counterfactual Recall Intelligence
Core research question: given the evidence recorded for a motor or lot, which different or additional evidence would have changed a recall finding or hold, and can that be answered without weakening the evaluator's conservative behavior?

Novelty: unverified. Feasibility: unverified. Differentiation: unverified.

## 2. Evidence Integrity and Contradiction Intelligence
Core research question: can recorded evidence be checked for internal contradiction, inconsistency or implausibility beyond hash-chain and schema integrity, and how should such findings be surfaced without turning suspicion into a clearance or a hold on their own?

Novelty: unverified. Feasibility: unverified. Differentiation: unverified.

## 3. Temporal Recall Revalidation
Core research question: how should earlier recall findings be re-evaluated, and their change over time shown, when new evidence, a changed recall scope or a later checkpoint arrives, while preserving the immutability of what was issued before?

Novelty: unverified. Feasibility: unverified. Differentiation: unverified.

## 4. Portable, Selectively Disclosed Evidence Packages
Core research question: can a recall finding and its supporting evidence be packaged so a third party can verify it offline while the holder discloses only the parts needed, and what can honestly be proven about a package that never leaves its issuer's trust boundary?

Novelty: unverified. Feasibility: unverified. Differentiation: unverified.

## 5. Recall Recovery Planner
Core research question: given a set of held motors, which remediation, re-inspection or evidence-gathering actions would resolve holds soonest or at least cost, and can the plan be produced without implying that the plan itself clears any motor?

Novelty: unverified. Feasibility: unverified. Differentiation: unverified.

## 6. Cross-Factory Federated Recall Intelligence
Core research question: can several independent factories share enough recall-relevant signal to find common affected lots or suppliers without pooling raw records, and what trust and custody assumptions would that require?

Novelty: unverified. Feasibility: unverified. Differentiation: unverified.
